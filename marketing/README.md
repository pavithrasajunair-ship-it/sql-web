# Marketing pack

Everything here is ready to post. Replace `LINK` with your live link (for example `https://pavithrasajunair-ship-it.github.io/sql-web/`).

## What is in this folder

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `daily/day-01.jpg … day-30.jpg` | 30 story-size (1080×1920) "question of the day" images. Post one per day. |
| `daily/schedule.md` | Per-day link, answer, explanation and caption. Each link opens that exact question in the app, with the answer revealed after a tap. |
| `cheatsheet-1.png`, `cheatsheet-2.png`, `Class12-SQL-cheatsheet.pdf` | One free cheat sheet (2 pages) to share as images or as a PDF. The PDF is also on the homepage. |
| `og.png` | The picture shown when the link is pasted into WhatsApp, Telegram, X or LinkedIn. |
| `make_assets.py` | Regenerates all of the above from the real question bank: `python3 marketing/make_assets.py`. |

## Launch plan (first 2 weeks)

1. **Day 0:** turn on GitHub Pages, open the link on your phone, and check that the link preview looks right when pasted into WhatsApp.
2. **Days 1–7:** post one question-of-the-day image per day (Instagram story, WhatsApp Status). Put `LINK` in your bio or a link sticker. Post the answer the next morning.
3. **Day 2:** share the cheat sheet in your class group and ask classmates to forward it.
4. **Day 3:** send the teacher message (below) to 3–5 Computer Science teachers.
5. **Day 4:** post the vertical launch film (`launch-vertical.mp4`) as a Reel or Short.
6. **Days 5–14:** ask users for one piece of feedback each and fix what they report. Keep posting the daily question.

## Tips
- Post at the same time every day; students check stories in the evening.
- Always put the link in the first line of the bio, because captions rarely get clicked.
- When someone shares their score card, thank them publicly. It encourages others to share.
- Ask for one specific thing ("which topic is hardest for you?"), not general feedback.
