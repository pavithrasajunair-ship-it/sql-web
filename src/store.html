Interview Q&A

Q: How do you find the second highest salary?

SELECT MAX(salary) AS second_highest
FROM employees
WHERE salary < (SELECT MAX(salary)
                 FROM employees);

second_highest
--------------
95000

The maximum of everything below the maximum. It copes with ties at the top.

C14. Customers who never ordered [Medium]

SELECT c.name
FROM customers c
WHERE NOT EXISTS (
  SELECT 1 FROM orders o
  WHERE o.cust_id = c.cust_id);

name
----
Lata

NOT EXISTS stays correct even when the subquery column can be NULL, unlike NOT IN.
